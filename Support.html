Support for the QMRS member app is currently being prepared.

App support

QMRS is preparing a dedicated mobile support experience for members, including access to current training information and QMRS location details.

The app will provide a convenient way for members to:

  • view current training schedules and updates;
  • access QMRS member information;
  • find QMRS locations and relevant site details;
  • receive important notifications and service updates.

Support information and contact details for the app will be published here once the service is live.
